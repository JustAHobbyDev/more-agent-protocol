# Commercial sensitivity

Project Cedar field studies are published to validate MORE, not to disclose the source project's competitive substance.

Anonymization and commercial-sensitivity review are separate gates. Removing names and identifiers is insufficient if the remaining content reveals proprietary findings, methods, decision logic, performance evidence, datasets, derived metrics, or operational know-how with plausible economic value.

The controlling policy is [`../../PUBLICATION_POLICY.md`](../../PUBLICATION_POLICY.md). When in doubt, publish the MORE lesson at a higher level and retain the exact source detail privately.
