# MORE Glossary

This glossary tracks project-specific acronyms and named abbreviations used by MORE. Standard software and Git/GitHub abbreviations such as API, PR, RC, and SHA are omitted unless MORE gives them a project-specific meaning.

## Acronyms

### MORE — Manager / Owner / Reviewer / Executor

The four conceptual responsibility and authority roles defined by the protocol:

- **Manager** — interprets intent, bounds work, coordinates handoffs, and reconciles results.
- **Owner** — holds consequential project authority and authorizes actions that exceed delegated authority.
- **Reviewer** — independently evaluates an exact candidate, package, artifact, result, or state.
- **Executor** — performs bounded work within the active authority and scope.

### IOC — Internal Orchestration Continuity

The rule that an active Manager should continue already-authorized internal orchestration across Executor and Reviewer handoffs until a genuine boundary is reached, rather than treating publication or receipt of an internal handoff as a reason to stop.

IOC does not expand authority, satisfy an Owner boundary, relax Reviewer independence, or bypass governing-work-item and latest-handoff rules.
